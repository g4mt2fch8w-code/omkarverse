SONAM/T-24 :
Zone : Moharli Core - Buffer
Female
Born: 2011
Father: Wagdoh
Mother: Madhuri


Sonam is one of the renowned Teliya sisters, who was known for overpowering her three siblings and conquering Teliya after her second litter got separated from her. Sonam was sighted mating with Bajrang and gave birth to her third litter of four cubs in 2020. Unfortunately, in 2021, one of the cubs went missing. After that in end 2021 her sub-adults separated from her and she was sighted in the month of February 2022 with her fourth litter with three young ones. Sonam, who is now over 14 years of age is nurturing her subadult cubs and is sighted occasionally in Moharli. Sonam lost her territory to Collarwali (female from Lara's litter). A female from Lara's second litter (Zara) was wandering in Sonam's territory and can prove to be a threat to her throne of Telia since she is younger and stronger than Sonam.
Sonam was seen in courtship with Chota Dadiyal in December 2022.

Sonam is being seen nearby Sitarampeth Area from 2023 with her Subadults and in January 2025 she came back to the Junona Buffer and Moharli Core Zone with 2 Subadult Cubs.

BALRAM/T-168 :

MALE
Born : 2015-2016
Zone : Kolara – Navegaon – Khutwanda – Moharli Core

TALA/T-100 :

MALE
Born: 2016
Father: Kakarghat Male
Mather: Shivanzhari female
Zone : used to sighted in Kolara – Navegaon – Khutwanda – Moharli Core, Kolara and Madnapur buffer now no more sighted.
Current Status : In Captivity, In transit centre


Tala was born in Kolsa region along with his three siblings. The four cubs and their mother were often sighted and became the star attraction in this area. These mischievous cubs grew up to be bold and were seen chasing the Indian Gaur, in Kolsa. During separation, Tala moved towards Moharli.

He then wandered into Moharli, where he was seen sitting with Sonam near by the lake, who at that time was raising cubs of 10-11 months of age. Bajrang, the dominant male of Moharli and who was father of Sonam's cubs pushed Tala out from Moharli. 

After a tough defeat by Bajrang, Tala moved to the Tadoba Range where he was often seen roaming in Matkasur's area. But Tala was not the only male who was invading Matkasur's territory his brother Rudra was also invading Matkasur's territory. However, after a few days, Matkasur was sighted injured which made it certain that Tala had challenged the dominant male of the area.

At the end of 2019, Tala was seen mating with Maya at Tadoba lake. He successfully pushed Matkasur out of his territory and he seen was roaming in half of the Matkasur's territory. Tala was the new King of the area and the father of Maya's fourth litter. From that only one cub survived to the adulthood who was Y-Mark male aka Sanju.

Later in year 2022 Tala was pushed out from the core area and he was seen invading into Matkasur's territory in Kolara and Madnapur buffer zone. He killed the cubs of Junabai and Matkasur in order to mate with Junabai and successfully had 2 cubs with her and Matkasur again lost his territory to Tala. Later Dagoba and Xylo both pushed Tala out from Kolara Madnapur buffer.

Tala then moved to Nimdhela-Ramdegi-Navegaon-Alizanza Buffer Zones in 2024, challenging the gentle giant Chota Matka where he got defeated bu him and after few days he was sighted again in the Tadoba Core Zone and then he disappeared

In 2025 January, He was captured by Forest Department as he had a fight with BMR-T-100 Male of Brahmapuri Division in Brahmapuri Forest Division.

Roma was born under the care of her mother Choti Tara and father Rudra. Roma and her 2 siblings were often sighted in Jamni as young cubs. Unfortunately, at the age of 8 months, her male sibling vanished and was never sighted after that.

Roma, the beautiful and bold young female now roams around Jamunbodi, the Tar road and some part of the non-tourism of the core area. During initial days after her separation she had face-off with the legendary tigress Maya.

In 2024, She gave birth to new cubs once again and this time this were 4 cubs fathered by Balram. It is said that none of them is alive and was killed by wild dogs or any other male tiger.

Female
Born: 2017
Father: Kali (Ghinaji)
Mother: P3
Zone : Navegaon Core & NIMDHELA-RAMDEGI-NAVEGAON-ALIZANZA BUFFERS

Babli was born during the year 2017 and raised in Alizanza and Navegoan under the gentle care of her mother P3 and father Kali aka Ghinaji, along with one male sibling. During monsoon 2024, Babli and Jharni had a fight for the territory. In 2024 she was seen mating with Chota Matka and may deliver new cubs in 2025. Babli is a beautiful female who is often sighted in Alizanza and Navegaon Meadows.

YUVRAJ/ PALAGAON MALE/ T-164 :

MALE
Born: 2018
Father: Shiva
Mother: Kuwani
Zone : Kolara Navegaon Moharli Khutwanda Core


Yuvraj is a young male who migrated to the Tadoba region from Kolsa. In 2021, Yuvraj was sighted after his separation, in the Tadoba range for at least a month. After which, he was not sighted for a while. Yuvraj is also half brother of tiger Mowgli.

Later, in 2022, he was sighted marking in Moharli for couple of months in Bajrang's territory between Khutwanda and Teliya lake. Towards the mid of the 2022's season, he returned to the Tadoba range and was being sighted at Jamni lake with Choti Tara and her daughter Bijli. Yuvraj proved to be a threat to Choti Tara's fifth litter as he was seen in courtship with her.

After the season opened in 2022, Yuvraj was sighted once in Kolara buffer after that which he was sighted at Khutwanda with some small injury marks on his face, then he returned back to Tadoba range where he was sighted a few times around Hilltop.

He fathered Bijli's first litter of 2 cubs and Choti Tara's 6th litter. Yuvraj protected his cubs from Mowgli when Mowgli wandered into Yuvraj's territory in 2023. He fought with Mowgli, defeated him and left Mowgli compelled to leave the area and he also had fight a with Chota Dadiyal. Yuvraj is doing good in 2024 in 97 Waterhole, Jamunbodi core area and Madnapur buffer range and proved to be one of the dominant males of core zone. He has also fathered cubs of Bijlee's 3 cubs in 2024.

Namdev was onginally known as Old Matkasur which soon, after his disappearance and New Martasur Male coming into picture, created some confusion.
Namdev was seen last time in 2016.

Yeda Anna / T-34 / Crooked tail :

MALE
FATHER : SULTAN


Yeda Anna dominated areas in the Moharli range since 2010. But the arrival of another male in the area, Wagdoh male meant that a fight would ensue. This inevitable fight berween the two heavyweights eventually took place in 2011. Post this, Yeda Anna shifted his territory more northwards, and started ruling areas in the Tadoba range. Post 2013, Yeda Anna's sightings drastically decreased. He was randomly seen a few times in 2015 in Tadoba range, but seemed like a tiger past his prime days. His last sighting was in the tourism area was in mid-2016.

His dead body was found at the start of 2018 in the periphery of the park, and it was confirmed that he had died because of old age and injuries sustained because of fights with other tigers. Yeda Anna was one of the biggest and quite the popular tiger during his prime days, and fathered around 20-25 cubs which ensured that his rich genes passed on and contributed to the gene pool. He was quite the temperamental tiger and was known to charge at tourist vehicles and thus he was fondly called 'Yeda Anna'. He fathered some of the famous tigers like Choti Tara, V7/Verrappan, Madhuri and Circiut family.


SOURCE : TIGERS OF TADOBA VOLUME 1 , BY JIGNESH JD PATEL.

Shivaji / T-35 : 

MALE
Place of Dominance: 2010 to 2013 in Kolsa Range 
Current status: Lust recorded in 2013

Shivaji ruled in the Kolsa range of the park, the southernmost limit of Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve. Since this area was not very popular with tourists at that time, not much was known of this male tiger. Shivaji was a big male and ruled large section of the forest. Along with his large territory, Shivaji had gained access to a lot of females as well.

During his short tenure as the dominant male in the area, Shivaji probably fathered cubs of Kakadghat female, HindiNala female and Panghat Kuti female. There have been no confirmed sightings of Shivaji since 2013, and he probably left the area after being pressurised by younger male tigers. Shivaji was one of the legendary tigers of Tadoba who gained true 'stardom ' during his time.

SOURCE : TIGERS OF TADOBA VOLUME 1 , BY JIGNESH JD PATEL.

Wagdoh Male / T-38 / Scarface :

MALE
FATHER : SULTAN

Wagdoh is one of the most famous tiger from Tadoba Andhari tiger reserve. He was considered to be one of the biggest male tigers in the entire Asia continent. Wagdoh was also kknown as Scarface he got a injury on his right eye while hunting the Indian gaur.  Wagdoh was consider to me migrated from the south of the Tadoba which is also known as Kolsa region. Wagdoh entered Moharli region in 2010. Wagdoh was a massive male tiger and successfully defeated Yeda Anna who was the dominant male tiger of the Moharli region. Wagdoh took control over the core area of Moharli such as Teliya lake and surrounding areas and he also managed to kill the cubs of the Madhuri female which were fathered by the Yeda Anna male. Wagdoh successfully mated with Madhuri in 2011 and also fathered the famous cubs who were also known as Teliya sisters (Sonam, Lara, Geeta and Mona). He also fathered the cubs of Kori female (Shiva male, Jharni female) in 2011. 

Later again in 2014 Wagdoh fathered cubs of Madhuri.Around 2015, Madhuri started to move towards the buffers of the Moharli and along with her Wagdoh also started to move towards the buffer and he left the prime area Teliya lake of the Tadoba. Male tiger named Khali was the dominant male tiger of the buffers at that time who successfully defeated Wagdoh and pushed him back. Later Wagdoh was seen in the buffer area with female known as Aishwarya and also fathered her cubs. Wagdoh was a caring father as he was seen most of the time spending with mates and cubs. Later due to arrival of new males in the Moharli region, Wagdoh had a tough time to maintain his territory and later he lost his grip and moved towards the Mamla buffers in 2020. Couple of times he was seen in the Mamla buffer feeding cattle kills around 2021. He was also seen mating with K Mark female tigress in 2021. Later in 2022, Wagdoh was found dead in the Mamla buffers due to old age.

Devdho female / T-8 / Aswalheera female :

Female
Current status: Last recorded in 2016
MOTHER : CHINCHGHAT female

Devdho female was the daughter of Chinchghat female and was born in 2009. Inherently a shy tigress, Devdho female was mainly seen on the Tar road around Khatoda Gate. She had her first litter of two cubs one male and one female in 2012, which was fathered by Yeda Anna. R-mark, her daughter took over Devdho female's territory. Her second litter was born in 2014 and comprising four cubs (three females and one male) was fathered by Wagdoh male. Bala is one of the female cub from this litter. Devdho female was last seen in 2016 along with three cubs this time fathered by Bajrang, but she hasn't been seen since 2016. She was most likely pushed out by her own daughter to gain control over the territory. During her prime, Devdho female's range extended from Tank No.9 to Khatoda gate and Khutwanda Raod to Kosaikanar Road.

SHE WAS SEEN LAST TIME IN 2016.
