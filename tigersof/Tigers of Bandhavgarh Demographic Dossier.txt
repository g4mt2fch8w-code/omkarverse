PREPARED FOR: Tiger Monitoring Repository & Landscape Research
LOCATION: Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve, Madhya Pradesh, Central India
CORE AREA: 716.903 sq. km (Tala, Magdhi, Khitauli Ranges)
BUFFER AREA: 820.035 sq. km (Dhamokhar, Panpatha, Johila Ranges)
TOTAL ADULT POPULATION ESTIMATE: 134+ Adult Tigers (>1 Year, 2022 AITE SECR Framework)


====================================================================================================
PART 1: CONTEMPORARY RESIDENT BREEDING COHORT (2020–2026)
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BAJRANG (T-Series Equivalent: Tala Dominant Male)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Male (M)
  * Nickname: "The Hulk of Bandhavgarh"
  * Primary Range: Core Tala Zone (Chakradhara, Bhitri, Ghorademon, Tala village fringes)
  * Lineage: Grandson of the legendary B2 through the Magdhi line.
  * Morphological Traits: Immense muscular body mass (~240-250 kg), prominent wide skull, bold sagittal
    facial crest, thick vertical throat ruff, bold black stripe branching like an inverted trident over
    the right brow.
  * Status & Behavior: Current undisputed territorial master of the central Tala core. Highly tolerant
    of safari jeeps; known for patrolling open meadow tracks and marking prominent Sal trees along the
    Charanganga crossing. Sired litters with Chakradhara, Sidhbaba, and Dotty's daughters.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PUJARI (T-Series Equivalent: Khitauli/Magdhi Dominant Male)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Male (M)
  * Nickname: "The Gentleman of the Jungle"
  * Primary Range: Khitauli and northern Magdhi Zones (Garhpuri, Umrar river, Tedka Munara)
  * Lineage: Offspring of the Mahaman lineage.
  * Morphological Traits: Elegant, slender yet tall frame, gentle and calm demeanor around vehicles,
    distinctive symmetrical facial stripes forming a religious "tilak/trident" mark over the forehead.
  * Fate & Succession: Reigned over Khitauli from 2019 to early 2026. Sired multiple litters with Tara,
    Darraha Bacchi, and Katiwah. In May 2026, Pujari was involved in an intense, protracted territorial
    battle with rival dominant male D1 over the Umrar riparian corridor and succumbed to injuries.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. JAMHOLE / JAMHOL (T-Series Equivalent: Magdhi/Khitauli Male)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Male (M)
  * Primary Range: Magdhi Zone, Khitauli periphery, and surrounding multiple-use buffer forests.
  * Morphological Traits: Stocky, heavy-shouldered male; battle scars across the left shoulder and muzzle;
    distinct jagged stripe resembling a jagged lightning bolt on the right flank.
  * Status & Behavior: Aggressive, powerful male frequently sighted in Magdhi; known for extensive
    movements across village buffers where he targets feral cattle. Consistently rivals D1 and Bajrang.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. D1 MALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Male (M)
  * Primary Range: Central and Northern Khitauli Core Zone (Garhpuri, Tedka Munara, Marjadgarh)
  * Morphological Traits: Large, aggressive, battle-tested prime male in his physical peak; missing the tip
    of his right ear from a territorial encounter; dark dorsal coloration.
  * Status: Ascended to primary dominance over Khitauli following territorial triumphs in 2025-2026.
    Controls access to resident breeding females Katiwah, Darraha Bacchi, and Tara.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DHAMOKAR / DM MALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Male (M)
  * Primary Range: Dhamokhar Buffer Zone, Kadewaha meadow, Mahaman periphery, extending into Tala & Magdhi.
  * Morphological Traits: Agile, cautious male; distinct "DM" or double-loop stripe pattern on his left flank.
  * Status: The dominant resident male of the Dhamokhar buffer tourism sector. Regularly bridges the gap
    between the core and buffer zones, fathering cubs with buffer-resident tigresses.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CHHOTA BHEEM (Historical Master of Khitauli)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Male (M)
  * Primary Range: Khitauli Core Zone (Tedka Munara, Umrar River, Nigaha)
  * Morphological Traits: Massive, barrel-chested male; famous for bold road strolls.
  * Life History: Reigned over Khitauli throughout the late 2010s to 2024. In November 2024, Chhota Bheem
    was rescued by the Forest Department after getting ensnared in a poacher's clutch wire loop around his
    throat in a buffer farm. Treated at Van Vihar National Park (Bhopal), he tragically succumbed to
    cardiac arrest in early February 2025.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DOTTY / DOTTY KRISHNA (T-17)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Official Code: T-17
  * Born: 2012 to Sookhi Patiha (T-5) and New Charger (T-6).
  * Primary Range: Tala and Magdhi Zones (Sookhi Talab, Dabhadhole, Climber's Point, Magdhi meadow).
  * Morphological Traits: Distinctive cluster of dot-like markings within the facial stripes over her brow
    (hence "Dotty"); sister of the famous Spotty (T-41).
  * Reproductive History: One of Bandhavgarh's greatest contemporary matriarchs. Mother of resident
    breeding females Sundari, Trident, and Trishuli. In 2022-2024, she successfully raised a litter of
    4 female cubs that dispersed across the reserve in late 2024.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. SUNDARI (T-Series Equivalent: Magdhi Resident Female)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Lineage: Daughter of Dotty (T-17).
  * Primary Range: Magdhi Core Zone (Dinosaur Rock, Sehra Dadra, Dabhadhole).
  * Status: Dominant resident female in Magdhi; highly tolerant of safari vehicles and active breeder.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. TRIDENT (T-Series Equivalent: Magdhi Resident Female)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Lineage: Daughter of Dotty (T-17).
  * Primary Range: Magdhi Zone (Murdhawa, Sookhi Talab).
  * Morphological Traits: Clear 3-pronged trident stripe on her forehead.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. TRISHULI (T-Series Equivalent: Magdhi Resident Female)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Lineage: Daughter of Dotty (T-17).
  * Primary Range: Magdhi Zone (Sukhi Patiha, Charger's Point).
  * Morphological Traits: Prominent "Trishul" shape above the right brow.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. SIDHBABA / SIDDHABABA FEMALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Primary Range: Core Tala Zone (Sidhbaba Temple meadow, Charanganga upstream, Bhitri).
  * Lineage: Descendant of the historic Chorbehra/Sidhbaba matriarchs.
  * Reproductive History: Raised a litter of 2 cubs in 2022–2023 that successfully separated in 2024;
    delivered a new litter of 2 cubs in mid-2024 fathered by Bajrang.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. CHAKRADHARA FEMALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Primary Range: Tala Zone (Chakradhara Meadow, Fort slopes, Ghorademon).
  * Lineage: Contemporary sovereign of the iconic Chakradhara meadow.
  * Reproductive History: Sired by Bajrang, she successfully raised a famous litter of 4 healthy cubs
    throughout 2024-2025, frequently photographed hunting chital across the marshy grassland.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. RAA FEMALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Primary Range: Northern Magdhi and Khitauli boundary (Garhpuri, Kumbhi Kachhar).
  * Morphological Traits: Natural Hindi Devanagari script marking "रा" (Raa) clearly visible in the
    forehead stripe pattern over her left eye.
  * Reproductive History: Delivered a new litter of 2 cubs in 2024.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. SEHRA FEMALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Primary Range: Magdhi Zone (Sehra Dadra, Mahaman dam).
  * Status: Prime breeding tigress; raised cubs in Sehra Dadra.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. DARRAHA BACCHI
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Primary Range: Khitauli Zone (Darraha nala, Umrar river).
  * Lineage: Daughter of the senior Darraha female.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16. KATIWAH FEMALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Primary Range: Khitauli Core Zone (Katiwah beat, Tedka Munara).
  * Reproductive History: Gave birth to 3 cubs in 2024. Faced territorial intrusion by an outside male

17. NADIWALI FEMALE

PART 2: MANAGEMENT ALPHANUMERIC IDENTIFIERS & RESEARCH REGISTER (T-SERIES)
====================================================================================================

T-1: "Panna Founder Matriarch" (Female)
  * Translocated from Bandhavgarh to Panna Tiger Reserve in March 2009.
  * Known as the "Mother of Panna" for giving birth to the first litters that revived the extinct
    Panna population.

T-2: "Panna Translocation Female" (Female)
  * Translocated from Bandhavgarh to Panna in 2009 alongside T-1.

T-3: "Panna Translocation Male" (Male)
  * Translocated to Panna to establish breeding pairs with T-1 and T-2.

T-5: "Sookhi Patiha Female" (Female)
  * Born 2006 to Baghdalka female and Bokha.
  * Iconic matriarch of Magdhi/Tala; mother of Spotty (T-41) and Dotty (T-17).

T-6: "New Charger" (Male)
  * Prominent dominant breeding male in Tala/Magdhi; sired Spotty and Dotty with T-5.

T-17: "Dotty / Dotty Krishna" (Female)
  * Born 2012 to T-5 and T-6. Key mother of contemporary Magdhi females.

T-18: "Jobi Male" (Male)
  * Prolific breeding male who fathered litters with T-24 (New Mahaman), T-40 (Chhoti Mahaman),
    and T-34 (Rajbahara).

T-20: "Jhurjhura Female" (Female)
  * Daughter of B2. Tragically killed by a speeding tourist safari vehicle in May 2010. Her 3 tiny
    orphaned cubs were rescued and hand-reared in an in-situ enclosure by park management.

T-21: "Dhamdhama Female" (Female)
  * Resident female of Khitauli; mother of Tara.

T-23: "Mahaman Female" (Female)
  * Matriarch of the Mahaman lineage; mother of T-24 and T-40.

T-24: "New Mahaman / Nigha Female" (Female)
  * Born 2009 to T-23. Reared multiple litters in northern Magdhi/Dhamokhar.

T-32: "Banvei Female" (Female)
  * Born 2007 to Old Banvei and the famous B2.

T-34: "Rajbahara Female" (Female)
  * Resident tigress of the Rajbahara dam meadow; mother of Solo (T-42).

T-35: "New Kankati" (Female)
  * Fierce resident tigress of Tala; tragically killed by an illegal electric wire snare along a buffer
    boundary in 2017.

T-36: "Old Kankati" (Female)
  * Matriarch with a damaged right ear; mother of T-37 (Bamera's Son).

T-37: "Bamera's Son" (Male)
  * Born 2011 to T-36 and Bamera (T-63). Popular, bold male; died naturally in November 2021 near Dhamokhar.

T-38: "Mukunda Male" (Male)
  * Large, dominant male holding territory across Magdhi and Khitauli in the mid-2010s.

T-40: "Chhoti Mahaman" (Female)
  * Born 2009 to T-23; key breeding tigress of Mahaman.

T-41: "Spotty" (Female)
  * Born 2012 to T-5 and T-6. Named for the distinct "T" stripe mark above her right eye. One of the
    most photographed tigresses in Bandhavgarh history; raised multiple litters in Tala core.

T-42: "Solo" (Female)
  * Born 2011 to T-34 and T-18. Legendary independent tigress of Tala.

T-63: "Bamera" (Male)
  * Massive, undisputed King of Bandhavgarh (2010–2015). Reigned over Tala and Magdhi; weighed ~240 kg.
    Died peacefully of old age in May 2016.

PART 3: HISTORICAL MONARCHS & ANCESTRAL LINEAGES (1990s–2010s)
====================================================================================================

1. CHARGER (The Legend - Reigned 1991 to 2000):
   - The most legendary wild tiger in Indian conservation history.
   - Known for his aggressive mock-charges towards safari elephants and jeeps, asserting total dominance.
   - Ruled the Tala core for a decade; fathered dozens of cubs with Sita and Mohini.
   - Died of old age in 2000; buried by foresters at 'Charger Point' in Magdhi zone where a memorial stands.

2. SITA (The Matriarch of Tala - 1990s):
   - The most photographed wild tigress in history; featured on the June 1997 cover of National Geographic.
   - Subject of groundbreaking BBC documentaries by Colin Stafford-Johnson.
   - Reared six successful litters in the Tala grasslands, establishing the genetic bedrock of modern Bandhavgarh.
   - Disappeared in 1998, likely falling victim to poachers outside the core.

3. LANGRU (Charger's Brother):
   - Named for his limp ('Langda'); held territory alongside Charger in the early 1990s.

4. B1, B2, B3 (The Three Princes):
   - Sired by Charger out of Sita's 1997 litter.
   - B2 (Sundar) emerged as the supreme ruler of Tala from 2000 to 2011, renowned for his colossal size,
     impassive calm, and fierce defense of Tala meadows against challenger males like Blue Eyes and Bokha.

5. MOHINI:
   - Daughter of Charger and Sita; tragically struck by a vehicle in 2009.

6. BOKHA & SHAMBHU:
   - Formidable adult males that contested Tala and Magdhi territories in the mid-2000s.

7. CHORBEHRA FEMALE & MIRCHAHNI FEMALE:
   - Historic matriarchs whose lineages populated the central Tala caves and northern ridges.

8. MOHAN (The Founding White Tiger - 1951):
   - Captured as a 9-month-old cub by Maharaja Martand Singh of Rewa in the Bandhavgarh forest.
   - Sole genetic progenitor of all white tigers in captive breeding programs globally.
