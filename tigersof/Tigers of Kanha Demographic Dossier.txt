DJ / Dhwajahandi :

Female Tigress 
Location : Kanha Tiger Reserve, Madhya Pradesh


DJ is the Female Tigress from Mukki Zone of Kanha Tiger Reserve and lives in the Tourism of Kanha Core, She is famous Among Tourist and one of the Important Tigress of Kanha Tiger Reserve. She is oftenly Sighted to Tourists during Jungle Safaris. Dj was Raising her cubs which seperated from her in 2024.
