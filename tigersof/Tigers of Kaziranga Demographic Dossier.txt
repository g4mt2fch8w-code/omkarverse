KAZIRANGA TIGER RESERVE'S TIGER PROFILE

Bhoismari :

Female Tigress 
Location : Kaziranga National Park & Tiger Reserve, Assam 


Bhoismari is the Female Tigress from Kaziranga Tiger Reserve and lives in the Tourism of Kaziranga, She is famous Among Tourist and one of the Important Tigress of Kaziranga National Park .She is oftenly Sighted to Tourists during Jungle Safaris.

Daffoli :

Female Tigress 
Location : Kaziranga National Park & Tiger Reserve, Assam 


Daffoli is the Female Tigress from Central Kaziranga Zone of Kaziranga Tiger Reserve and lives in the Tourism of Kaziranga, She is famous Among Tourist and one of the Important Tigress of Kaziranga National Park .She is oftenly Sighted to Tourists during Jungle Safaris. Daffoli Female is very bold Tigress and She was said to be pregnant in October 2024, She might have delivered Cubs in Early 2025.
