----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MT-1 (The Satpura Sultan - Dominant Founder Patriarch)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Code: MT-1 / MDHV_1 / T-101
  * Sex: Male (M)
  * Age at Capture: Approximately 4.0 years (Prime Adult)
  * Provenance: Wild-born in Satpura Tiger Reserve (Hoshangabad/Narmadapuram, MP).
  * Capture & Release Date: Captured early March 2023; translocated and formally released into 
    Soft-Release Enclosure-1 on March 10, 2023 by CM Shivraj Singh Chouhan & Union Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia.
  * Telemetry Equipment: Fitted with an advanced VHF/GPS-satellite tracking collar.
  * Morphological Keys: Massive, muscular frame; deep rich orange coat; prominent symmetrical "V-shaped" 
    marking above the right eye; three bold vertical stripes on the left ribcage merging into a Y-fork; 
    thick black tail tip with 7 distinct rings.
  * Territorial Range: Commands an expansive territory of ~180 sq. km encompassing the George Castle 
    plateau, Sakhya Sagar lake perimeter, Churrat valley, and extending south along the Maniyari river.
  * Reproductive Contribution: Confirmed sire of MT-3's landmark September 2024 litter (2 cubs) and 
    MT-4's 2026 litter, establishing the foundational genetic pedigree of the revived Madhav population.

  * Provenance: Wild-born in Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve (Umaria, MP).

3. MT-3 (Madhav Rani - Founder Female 2 / Breeding Matriarch)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Code: MT-3 / MDHV_3 / T-103
  * Sex: Female (F)
  * Age at Capture: Approximately 2.5–3.0 years
  * Provenance: Wild-born in Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve (Umaria, MP).

  * Historical Significance: **The First Mother of Modern Madhav**. Successfully conceived, gestated, 
    and birthed two wild cubs in September 2024, ending a 27-year hiatus of wild tiger reproduction.

PART 2: THE FIRST WILD-BORN GENERATION (SEPTEMBER 2024 LITTER)
====================================================================================================

In September 2024, motion-triggered camera traps within a secluded sandstone ravine bordering Sakhya 
Sagar photographed tigress MT-3 escorting two young cubs, confirmed to be approximately 2 to 2.5 months old. 
Subsequent field tracking confirmed their paternity by resident male MT-1.

PART 4: REGIONAL DISPERSALS & METAPOPULATION CORRIDOR TRANSIENTS
====================================================================================================

  1. T-DISP-1 (Kuno-Pohari Transient Tiger):
     - In late 2021 and early 2022, prior to the reintroduction launch, field teams recorded pugmarks 
       and scat of a dispersing male tiger moving through the Pohari territorial forest corridor 
       connecting Kuno National Park to northern Madhav.
  2. T-DISP-2 (Ranthambore-Chambal Migrant):
     - Photo-documented during AITE 4th/5th cycles dispersing across the Chambal ravines into the 
       Sheopur-Shivpuri forest continuum, demonstrating natural corridor permeability.


====================================================================================================
PART 5: HISTORICAL TIGERS OF MADHAV (PRE-EXTIRPATION ARCHIVES)
====================================================================================================

  1. T-HIST-1 (The George Castle Royal Hunt Tiger - December 1911):
     - Documented in the royal hunting logs of the Scindia Court during the visit of King George V. 
       A massive prime male recorded in the Sultanpur/George Castle forest tract.
  2. T-HIST-2 (Sakhya Sagar Historic Matriarch - Circa 1980s):
     - Resident breeding tigress that reared multiple litters along the Sakhya Sagar reedbeds during 
       the early years of Madhav National Park.
  3. T-HIST-3 (Madhav Sagar Resident Male - Circa 1990s):
     - Prime male that defended the lake basin through the late 1990s; one of the last stable territorial 
       males before the poaching and fragmentation crisis.
  4. T-HIST-6 (The Last Resident Wild Tiger - Extirpated ~2010–2012):
     - Solitary male whose pugmarks and scrapes were documented by forest guards until 2011/2012, after 
       which no resident signs were detected until the 2023 reintroduction.

      - MT-2: Black collar strap with VHF whip antenna.
