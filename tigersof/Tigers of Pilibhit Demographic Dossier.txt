S3 is the Male Tiger from of Pilibhit Tiger Reserve and lives in the Tourism of Pilibhit, he is often sighted to Tourists during Jungle Safaris ,He is most famous Among Tourist and one of the Important Tiger of Pilibhit Tiger Reserve. S3 is Cute and Mostly Sighted Tiger, He is also called as Tree Lover as he love to Hug Tree multiple Times. S3 is also called as Kulhadiram and it is said that he migrated to Pilibhit from Different Forest, He have a Axe Mark on his Face and his size also very big. S3 and S2 had a Fight in 2024 in which S3 was injured, S3 often changes his location to do Cattle Kills.
