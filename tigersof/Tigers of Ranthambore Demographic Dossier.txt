Riddhi / T124

Female Tigress 
Location : Ranthambhore Tiger Reserve, Rajasthan 
Mother : Arrowhead Tigress 


Riddhi is the Female Tigress from of Ranthambhore Tiger Reserve and lives in the Zone 2,3 and 4 Tourism Zone of Ranthambhore Core, She is often sighted to Tourists during Jungle Safaris, She is famous Amongst Tourist and one of the Important Tigress of Ranthambhore Tiger Reserve. Riddhi gave birth to cubs in 2023 by mating with T 108 Male Tiger Ganesh. She is often sighted nearby the Rajbagh and Padam Lakes of Ranthambhore, The Riddhi Tigress was raising her 3 cubs in 2025 and will separate in 2025 only. Riddhi was daughter of Arrowhead Tigress born in 2020. Riddhi and her Sister Siddhi (T125) had intense fights with each other and even Riddhi and Arrowhead were engaged in fights with each other after separation.  Riddhi is one of the Boldest, Fierce and Beautiful Tigress of India. The Famous Tigress of India Machhli was the Grandmother of Riddhi Tigress.
