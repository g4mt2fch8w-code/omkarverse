                        TIGERS OF SATPURA: OFFICIAL DEMOGRAPHIC DOSSIER,
              LONGITUDINAL MONITORING REGISTERS, BREEDING MATRILINES & FIELD IDENTIFICATIONS

PREPARED FOR: Tiger Monitoring Repository & Biosphere Carnivore Research Archive
LOCATION: Satpura Tiger Reserve & Pachmarhi Biosphere Reserve, Madhya Pradesh, Central India
CORE AREA: 1,339.264 sq. km (Kamti, Bori, Churna, East Pachmarhi, West Pachmarhi, Matkuli Ranges)
BUFFER AREA: 794.040 sq. km (Hoshangabad, Rampur Bhatodi Project, and West Chhindwara Divisions)
STATUS: Central India's Mountain Tiger Stronghold & Founder Donor for Regional Reintroduction


====================================================================================================
DEMOGRAPHIC CENSUS SUMMARY: THE RECOVERY TIMELINE (2014–2026)
====================================================================================================
  * AITE 2014 (Cycle 3): 16 Unique Adult Tigers identified (early post-village relocation baseline).
  * AITE 2018 (Cycle 4): 40 Adult Individuals identified from 911 camera-trap detections (SECR Density: 
    1.39 ± 0.20 tigers / 100 sq. km). Highest densities recorded in Bori and Kamti ranges.
  * AITE 2022 (Cycle 5): **50 Unique Adult Tigers** captured (830 camera stations, 26,761 trap nights; 
    SECR Density surged to **2.01 ± 0.28 tigers / 100 sq. km**).
  * 2024–2026 Contemporary Status: Total resident population exceeds **65–70+ Wild Tigers** across core 
    and buffer zones, with active multi-female breeding in Churna, Mallupura, Bori, and Madhai.

PART 2: FOUNDER DONOR ROLE: MALE MT-1 AND THE MADHAV REINTRODUCTION
====================================================================================================

On **March 10, 2023**, Satpura Tiger Reserve played a historic role in national wildlife restoration:
  * **Tiger MT-1 (The Satpura Sultan)**: A 4-year-old prime wild male was safely captured in Satpura's core, 
    fitted with a satellite telemetry collar, and air-lifted/translocated to **Madhav National Park (Shivpuri)**.
  * Historic Success: Released into Madhav alongside females from Bandhavgarh, MT-1 established complete dominance, 
    fathering the landmark September 2024 litter of wild cubs that resurrected tigers in northern MP after 
    27 years of extinction.

PART 5: HISTORICAL & FORESTRY ARCHIVES (CAPTAIN FORSYTH ERA)
====================================================================================================

  * **T-HIST-1 (Captain Forsyth Bori Male - 1865)**: Documented by Captain James Forsyth during the creation 
    of India's first forest reserve in Bori.
  * **T-HIST-2 (Pachmarhi Plateau Resident - 1910s)**: Historical shikar logs record large solitary tigers 
    inhabiting the high ravines below Dhoopgarh.
  * **T-HIST-3 (Denwa Valley Matriarch - 1980s)**: Resident tigress that roamed the Denwa river prior to the 
    construction of the Tawa Dam reservoir.
