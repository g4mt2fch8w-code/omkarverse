F2 Tigress :


FEMALE
Zone : Gothangaon 
Father : Surya Male Tiger
Mother : Fairy Tigress

The Famous Tigress F-2 is from Gothangaon Zone of Umred-Karhandla-Paoni Wildlife Sanctuary, she's Grand daughter of Mayya Tigress of Tadoba by Father's Bloodline and Grand daughter of Jay Tiger by Mother's Side. F2's litters includes 5 Cubs from 2nd litter and her first last litter were having 2 Cubs killed by N4 Male Tiger. She is often sighted to Tourists during Jungle Safaris , She is  famous Among Tourist and one of the Important Tigress of Umred-Karhandla-Paoni Wildlife Sanctuary.
