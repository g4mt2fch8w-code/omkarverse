TIPESHWAR WILDLIFE SANCTUARY

________________________________


Talabvali :

The Old Aged Tigress from Tourism Zone of Tipeshwar Wildlife Sanctuary, Talavwali, T-2 gone missing in the Monsoons of 2024 , Talavwali had very famous Litters some cubs stayed in Tipeshwar and some of them migrated to other Territorial Forests of Yavatmal,Chandrapur, Nanded Districts and Telangana, T-2 was -mother of famous Daisy Tigress and Archi Tigress, both tigress are tourist friendly and bold like her mother !

________________________________



Archie : 

Zone : Sunna-Mathani-Kodori
Born : July 2016
Mother : Talavwali (T2)
Father : Star Male

Archie is the Famous Tigress from Tourism Zone of Tipeshwar and had 4 Cubs born in 2023, 2 of them were said to be no more and 2 other Cubs are still in Tipeshwar Wildlife Sanctuary, they are - Chhava (Rocky Male) and Sitara Female ,. Her first litter is from Zanjeer Male of Tipeshwar which oftenly goes to Telangana and after 2024 Season Opener he was missing and not sighted.

Veer : 

Zone Sunna-Mathani-Kodori 
Born : October 2018
Mother : Pilkhan 

Veer is the Son of Pilkhan Tigress of Tipeshwar Wildlife Sanctuary, he was born in October 2018, He is now mostly Sighted in 2025 during Jungle Safaris and was seen spending Time with Archi Tigress.

________________________________

Zanjeer  :

Zone : Sunna-Mathani-Kodori 
Born : February 2018
Mother : 4 Mark (T22)


Zanjeer is the Male Tiger from Tourism Zone of Tipeshwar Wildlife Sanctuary, he was one of the 3 cubs of 4Mark Tigress, he went missing since Starting of Tourism Season of Tipeshwar Wildlife Sanctuary, may be he migrated to Telangana or other Forest Parts.

________________________________

Avenger  :

Zone : Sunna-Mathani-Kodori 
Born : March 2022
Mother : 4 Mark

Avenger is the Young Male Tiger borm inside Tourism Zone of Tipeshwar Wildlife Sanctuary, he then migrated in his Subadult age to Dharashiv ans Solapur Districts of Maharashtra, his another name is "Tadu" and a latest news says that forest department was planning to send him to Sahyadri Tiger Reserve.

________________________________

Star Male :

Not much data is known of Star Male as he was seen a long ago but he is fathers of Archi's Cubs and is now missing.

4 Mark (T-22) :

Zone : Sunna-Mathani-Kodori 
Born : November 2013
Mother : Pilkhan Tigress 

4 Mark Tigress is from Tourism Zone of Tipeshwar Wildlife Sanctuary, she has been mother of 4 Litters till the data and her new ones were seen in February 2025, She was having 3 Cubs in First,Second and Fourth Litter and 4 Cubs in her Third Litter.
